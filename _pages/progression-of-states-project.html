02 BEYOND RETAINING HISTORY

What is true now? What is still missing?

Memory makes past evidence available. A belief state makes the agent's current understanding explicit, together with what it still needs to learn or accomplish.

WORLD STATE

What the agent currently understands

Task-relevant entities, their states, and their relations, with provenance, confidence, and supporting evidence when available.

EPISTEMIC GAP

What it still needs to learn

An unresolved question or a claim that needs stronger evidence, such as distinguishing database waiting from JVM-side processing.

ACHIEVEMENT GAP

What it still needs to change

A difference between the current world and the goal, such as making the mug hot before placing it in the cabinet.

03 MAINTAIN. VALIDATE. RECOVER.

Keep the belief useful.

CONSISTENCY COMES FIRST

Before measuring progress, check the belief.

A candidate update can contain conflicting states or claims unsupported by the interaction evidence. The Belief Sentinel returns issues for revision; only the validated belief becomes the next decision context.

Two incompatible states in one update.

The candidate records the microwave as both open and closed. It is not ready to guide the next action.

Schematic update illustrating Section 3.2, not a recorded case step.

BELIEF TRAPPING

Three ways to keep acting without moving forward.

01

Static

SSS

Actions continue, but the relevant world state stays unchanged.

Escape: stop the ineffective transition.
02

Cycle

ABA

The state changes, then returns to a previously visited state.

Escape: break the recurrent transition.
03

Drift

Gap ?

New information changes the belief, but not the part needed to resolve the active gap.

Escape: return attention to the active gap.

Schematic patterns from Section 3.3. Detection combines gap persistence, progress stagnation, and belief recurrence across a window of validated transitions.

FACTORIZED RECOVERY

Escape the pattern. Address the gap.

Recovery must say both how to break the unproductive pattern and what the next action or investigation needs to resolve.

Do not repeat the ineffective action under the unchanged belief.

+

Acquire evidence that distinguishes the remaining explanations.

The active gap stays fixed during recovery. The task agent selects the action; the constraints do not supply an answer or a predetermined tool call.
